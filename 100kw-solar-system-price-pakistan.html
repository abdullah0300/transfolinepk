05   FAQ KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently asked questions: 100 kW solar in Pakistan.

Have technical or financial questions about your commercial solar transition? Our engineering team answers the most critical queries below.

COST What is the total turnkey cost of a 100kW commercial solar system in Pakistan?

In 2026, a complete turnkey 100kW commercial solar plant costs between PKR 7.5 Million to PKR 9.2 Million depending on roof structure design (elevated L3/L4 vs standard), inverter brand (Huawei SUN2000 vs Sungrow), and green metering fees. With annual bill savings over PKR 7.2 Million, payback occurs in just 2.0 to 2.2 years.

SAVINGS How much electricity bill savings does a 100kW solar system generate monthly?

A 100kW system generates 11,500 to 12,500 units per month, producing over 140,000 units annually. Based on Pakistan's commercial peak and off-peak tariffs averaging PKR 52–58/unit, this eliminates PKR 600,000 to PKR 720,000 every month from your utility bill.

SPACE How much shadow-free roof space is needed for a 100kW solar installation?

Using high-efficiency 585W Tier-1 bifacial panels, a 100kW system requires approximately 7,500 to 8,500 square feet of unobstructed rooftop or ground space. We can elevate the structure to preserve full rooftop usability for parking or storage.

GRID What happens to solar power generation during WAPDA load shedding?

By NEPRA safety regulations, standard on-grid inverters anti-island (shut off) during grid outages to protect linemen. However, TransfoLine can integrate a smart DG-PV synchronization controller that allows your 100kW solar plant to run safely alongside your backup diesel generator, cutting diesel fuel usage by up to 70%.

NOC How long does NEPRA net-metering approval and green meter installation take?

TransfoLine manages the entire DISCO liaison (LESCO, K-Electric, IESCO, FESCO, MEPCO). The process takes 4 to 6 weeks from initial PEC design submission to live bi-directional green meter synchronization.

TX HOOKUP How does the 100kW solar system connect to our existing transformer and LT panel?

The 100kW 3-phase string inverter outputs 400V AC power, which connects directly to your main LT distribution panel through a dedicated 200A 4-Pole breaker and bi-directional energy meter before your step-down distribution transformer.
