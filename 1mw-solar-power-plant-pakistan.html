05   MEGA-WATT KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently asked questions: 1 MW to 5 MW solar plants.

Detailed engineering and commercial answers for industrial managing directors, chief financial officers, and plant engineers.

COST What is the total turnkey cost of a 1 MW industrial solar power plant in Pakistan?

In 2026, a turnkey 1 MW industrial solar plant in Pakistan costs between PKR 72 Million to PKR 88 Million. This comprehensive package includes 1,710+ Tier-1 TopCon modules, 100kW/250kW inverters, our custom-built 1.25 MVA (0.8kV/11kV) step-up transformer, 11kV VCB switchgear, civil work, and full NEPRA net-metering approvals, resulting in full capital payback in just 2.0 years.

1.25 MVA TX Why is a 1.25 MVA dual-secondary step-up transformer required for a 1 MW plant?

Injecting 1 MW of power at 400V or 800V creates immense electrical current (over 1,400 Amperes), leading to massive cable resistive losses and dangerous heat buildup. A 1.25 MVA step-up transformer converts inverter voltage to 11kV or 33kV, slashing transmission losses, providing electrostatic Faraday shielding, and meeting DISCO grid interconnection standards.

SAVINGS How much annual electricity savings does a 1 MW solar plant generate?

A 1 MW solar plant generates between 1,450,000 to 1,520,000 kWh per year. Based on Pakistan's industrial B-3 tariffs averaging PKR 52–58/unit, this produces over PKR 75 Million to PKR 86 Million in direct annual electricity bill savings.

SPACE How much roof area or ground land is needed for a 1 MW solar plant?

For industrial shed rooftop installations, a 1 MW system requires approximately 75,000 to 85,000 square feet of unobstructed roof space using 585W–600W Tier-1 bifacial panels. For ground-mounted installations, it requires approximately 2.5 to 3.0 acres of level land.

WHEELING Can we wheel 1 MW solar power to our factory from an off-site solar farm?

Yes. Under NEPRA's competitive trading bilateral contract market (CTBCM) regulations, industrial companies can install 1 MW to 50 MW off-site solar plants and wheel clean electricity through NTDC/DISCO 11kV/132kV transmission grids directly to their factories. TransfoLine manages the complete bilateral wheeling study, interconnection design, and DISCO approval.

GENSET Can a 1 MW solar plant synchronize with our heavy diesel/gas generators during load shedding?

Yes. We install advanced DG-PV reverse-power protection controllers that enable the 1 MW solar plant to generate power simultaneously with heavy gas/diesel generators during grid outages, reducing factory fuel consumption by up to 70% without genset reverse-power tripping.
