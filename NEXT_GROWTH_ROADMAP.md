# TransfoLine Growth & Strategic Roadmap

This document outlines upcoming growth initiatives, engineering tools, and B2B expansion tracks for TransfoLine.

---

## 📌 Track 1: Interactive Engineering & Sizing Calculators
* **Transformer kVA Factory Load Calculator**:
  - Live JavaScript tool on ew-used-transformers.html\ and \	ransformer-repair.html\.
  - Computes exact kVA rating based on connected factory machinery HP, monthly peak units (kWh), and operating Power Factor (PF).
  - Generates instant customized BOM and sends one-click lead data to WhatsApp/sales.
* **Substation Capex & Payback Estimator**:
  - Estimates cost and equipment breakdown for 11kV grid connection, VCB switchgear, transformer, and DISCO approval timelines.

1. **Sundar Industrial Estate (SIE), Lahore** — Heavy transformer repair, rewinding & HT panels.

## 📌 Track 3: EV Infrastructure & Fast-Charging Network Expansion
* **Dedicated EV Substation Packages**:
  - Turnkey pad-mounted transformer stations tailored for 60kW, 120kW, 180kW, and 240kW DC ultra-fast chargers.
* **Motorway & Highway Corridor Charging**:
  - Turnkey power infrastructure for fuel stations on M-2, M-3, M-4, and M-5 motorways.
* **Commercial Plaza & Fleet Depots**:
  - Level 2 AC chargers + Level 3 DC fast chargers with load-sharing transformers and OCPP cloud billing integration.

---

## 📌 Track 4: SEMrush Research & New Technical Content Clusters
* **HT/LT Switchgear & Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCB)**:
  - Technical guides on 11kV VCB maintenance, protection relays (Overcurrent & Earth Fault), and busbar sizing.
* **Transformer Oil BDV & Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA)**:
  - Preventive maintenance guides explaining IEEE/IEC limits for moisture ppm, dielectric breakdown voltage, and gas trends (acetylene, methane).
* **Industrial Solar Synchronization & Zero-Export Controllers**:
  - In-depth engineering case studies for factories running DG-GenSet + Solar + WAPDA synchronized microgrids.
