4-Step Emergency Safety Isolation Protocol for Multan Sub-Stations

When an industrial transformer trips at a plant in Multan Industrial Estate, Vehari Road, or Khanewal Road, follow this mandatory safety isolation protocol:

  1. Isolate 11 KV HT Breaker: Trip the HT Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) panel and rack out the breaker handle. Apply Lockout/Tagout (LOTO).
  2. Open LT Main Breaker: Disconnect the Low-Tension (415V) main Air Circuit Breaker (ACB) to prevent back-feeding.
  3. Inspect Buchholz Relay & Pressure Relief Device (PRD): Check for gas accumulation or oil spillage near the conservator tank.
  4. Call Emergency Engineering Support: Call TransfoLine's emergency repair hotline at 0314 4641288. Explore our Multan location hub page.

99.99% Pure Copper Coil Rewinding & Insulation Specs

TransfoLine rewinds damaged transformer coils using superior engineering materials:

  • 100% Pure Electrolytic Copper: High-conductivity copper strip (99.99% purity) to minimize electrical losses ($I^2R$) and heat generation.
  • Nomex & Kraft Insulation: High-dielectric layer insulation wrapped between high-voltage (HV) and low-voltage (LV) coils.
  • Axial Duct Spacers: Preserves oil circulation channels between coil layers for maximum summer heat dissipation.

Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI) & Core Baking

After coiling, the assembly undergoes Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI):

The core and coils are baked in a vacuum oven to remove all residual moisture, then submerged in high-grade insulating varnish under pressure. This locks coil turns firmly, preventing mechanical vibration hum and short-circuit displacement. See our transformer repair service page.

Post-Repair Routine Testing & Commissioning Protocol

Before any repaired transformer leaves our facility for Multan, it passes 5 mandatory routine tests:

  • Turns Ratio & Vector Group Test: Confirms correct voltage transformation ratio across all tap settings.
  • Winding Resistance Test: Measures DC resistance across phases to verify balanced copper turns.
  • Megger Insulation Test: Tests insulation resistance between HV-LV, HV-Earth, and LV-Earth (10,000 MΩ+).
  • Oil BDV Breakdown Test: Verifies fresh mineral oil dielectric strength (>60 kV).
  • No-Load & Full-Load Loss Test: Validates efficiency and core losses against WAPDA/MEPCO limits. Read our used vs repair decision guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What immediate safety steps must a factory team take when a transformer trips in Multan?

Immediately open the 11 KV HT VCB breaker, rack out the breaker handle, lock out tag out (LOTO), test for zero voltage on LT distribution main busbars, and inspect Buchholz relay gas accumulation before attempting reclosure.

Does TransfoLine use 99.99% pure copper wire for transformer coil rewinding in Multan?

Yes. TransfoLine rewinds all industrial transformers using 100% guaranteed 99.99% pure electrolytic copper wire insulated with Nomex paper, treated under Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI) for maximum mechanical and thermal strength.
