The 4-Step IESCO Industrial Connection Approval Workflow

To energize a new industrial facility in Rawalpindi (Rawat, IJP Road, Saddar), factory owners must complete IESCO's technical inspection workflow:

  1. Sanction Load Application: Submit load demand calculations and single-line diagram (SLD) prepared by a certified electrical engineer.
  2. Substation Inspection: IESCO field engineers inspect the transformer, HT VCB panel, earthing pit resistance (<1 Ohm), and high-voltage room clearance.
  3. Routine Test Report Verification: Verification of factory routine test certificates (Megger, turns ratio, BDV) issued by an accredited laboratory.
  4. Metering Bay Installation: Final sealing of IESCO CT/PT metering bay and energization. Explore our Rawalpindi location hub page.

11 KV HT Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) Panel Standards

IESCO mandates an 11 KV Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) HT panel for industrial sanction loads exceeding 500 KVA. Key switchgear specifications include:

  • Vacuum Interrupter Bottle: Arc quenching in high-vacuum chamber for ultra-fast fault clearance (<50ms).
  • Microprocessor Protection Relays: Dual overcurrent (50/51) and earth fault (50N/51N) protective relays.
  • Current & Potential Transformers (CT/PT): Metering class CT/PT units calibrated to IESCO billing accuracy standards.

LT Switchgear & Power Factor Improvement (PFI) Plants

IESCO strictly monitors industrial power factor in Rawalpindi. If your plant's power factor drops below 0.90 lagging due to heavy motor loads in Rawat or IJP Road, IESCO applies severe monthly billing surcharges.

TransfoLine fabricates automated **PFI Capacitor Panels** equipped with heavy-duty micro-farad capacitors and microprocessor power factor controllers that automatically switch capacitor steps to maintain a continuous 0.95 to 0.98 power factor. See our HT/LT panel installation page.

Top 4 Technical Reasons IESCO Rejects Substation Applications

IESCO inspection teams reject industrial connections for the following technical flaws:

  1. Missing Routine Test Certification: Sourcing secondhand uncertified transformers without official routine test reports.
  2. High Earthing Pit Resistance: Earth pit resistance exceeding 1.0 Ohm.
  3. Incorrect Vector Group: Using Yyn0 transformers instead of IESCO-mandated Dyn11. Read our Rawalpindi price & spec guide.
  4. Under-Sized Neutral Conductors: Using substandard neutral cabling that overheats under heavy motor loads. Read our Rawat heavy transformer guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What HT switchgear is required by IESCO Rawalpindi Circle for industrial connections?

IESCO Rawalpindi Circle mandates an 11 KV Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) HT panel equipped with overcurrent and earth fault protection relays, potential transformers (PT), current transformers (CT), and an approved metering bay prior to grid energization.

Why is a Power Factor Improvement (PFI) panel mandatory for Rawalpindi factories?

IESCO imposes heavy financial penalties on industrial consumers if plant power factor drops below 0.90 lag. Installing an automated PFI capacitor panel maintains power factor at 0.95–0.98, reducing transformer kVA load and eliminating utility surcharges.
