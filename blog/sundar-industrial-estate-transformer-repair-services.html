Industrial Profile: Power Demands in Sundar & Multan Road

Sundar Industrial Estate (SIE), managed by the Punjab Industrial Estates Development and Management Company (PIEDMC), represents Pakistan's premier planned manufacturing zone. Boasting over 500 operating factories, the estate houses massive pharmaceutical manufacturing complexes, paper and board converters, masterbatch polymer processors, and international FMCG distribution hubs. Extending north along the historic Multan Road corridor toward Manga Mandi, heavy chemical, textile processing, and metal fabricating plants operate round the clock.

Unlike residential distribution grids, electrical substations in Sundar face unique operational stresses:

  • Zero Tolerance for Harmonic Distortion: Pharmaceutical computerized cleanrooms and automated blister packaging machines rely on sensitive PLC microprocessor controls that cannot tolerate voltage sags, harmonics, or power outages.
  • Continuous Multi-Day Chemical Batches: In pharmaceutical fluid-bed dryers, chemical reactors, and polymer extrusion lines, a 30-minute power disruption causes active ingredients to solidify inside barrels, requiring days of mechanical disassembly and millions of PKR in raw material scrappage.
  • High Ambient Temperatures & Dust Ingress: Located on the semi-arid plains southwest of Lahore, industrial units face intense ambient heat (45°C) and heavy particulate dust that coats transformer radiator fins and contaminates silica gel breathers.

On-Site High-Vacuum Oil Filtration Without Substation De-Energization

In many Sundar manufacturing plants, transformer oil has suffered dielectric degradation (BDV dropping below 30 kV and moisture exceeding 35 ppm), but the plant cannot afford to shut down production for a 2-day oil overhaul. TransfoLine provides specialized Online High-Vacuum Oil Filtration.

Our mobile vacuum oil treatment plant connects to the upper and lower valves of your transformer while energized or during a planned 4-hour scheduled shift turnover:

  1. Oil is drawn from the bottom valve, heated to 65°C under computerized thermostatic control, and atomized into a two-stage high-vacuum degassing chamber (< 0.05 mbar).
  2. Dissolved moisture, volatile combustible hydrocarbon gases, and air bubbles are flash-evaporated and vented to the atmosphere.
  3. The oil passes through a multi-stage 0.5-micron micro-fiber filter bank that captures sub-micron carbon sludge, metallic particles, and fibrous debris.
  4. Clean, dry, degassed insulating oil is pumped back into the conservator tank at the top, restoring dielectric breakdown voltage from an unsafe 22 kV to a pristine > 65 kV in a continuous closed loop.

In high-throughput manufacturing environments like Sundar Industrial Estate, electrical loads are characterized by rapid duty-cycle shifts and continuous thermal dissipation. For instance, in automated blow-molding and pharmaceutical bottle-forming plants, hydraulic clamping motors and electric heating bands switch on and off cyclically hundreds of times per hour. This cyclic pulse-loading induces severe electromagnetic mechanical vibrations inside the transformer windings, causing micro-frictional wear against the interlayer paper insulation.

Furthermore, because many manufacturing units in Sundar operate dedicated 11kV rooftop solar grid-tied systems alongside LESCO utility supply, reverse power flow during midday low-production hours frequently drives local substation voltages upward. When utility line voltage surges coincide with peak solar generation, the transformer core operates near saturation, accelerating internal gas generation and thermal degradation.
