IESCO Grid Specifications & CDA Safety Standards

To connect a transformer to IESCO (Islamabad Electric Supply Company) 11 KV lines in Islamabad Capital Territory, your installation must satisfy key criteria:

  • Dyn11 Vector Group: Mandatory delta-star vector group for balanced neutral distribution.
  • Off-Load Tap Changer (±5%): Standard 5-step tap changer for voltage regulation during grid fluctuations.
  • CDA Noise & Indoor Safety Standards: Low-decibel acoustic design and oil containment bunds for indoor installations in Blue Area commercial plazas.

Why is buying direct from TransfoLine cheaper than local Islamabad dealers?
