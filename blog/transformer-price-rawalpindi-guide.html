IESCO Rawalpindi Circle Specifications & Test Requirements

To connect a transformer to IESCO (Rawalpindi Circle) 11 KV distribution lines, your installation must meet strict technical standards:

  • Dyn11 Vector Group: Delta high-voltage primary with Star grounded low-voltage neutral for stable 415V 3-phase power.
  • Off-Load Tap Changer (±5%): Standard 5-step tap changer allowing voltage adjustment during peak IESCO grid voltage sags.
  • Routine Test Certification: Every unit must ship with insulation resistance (Megger), turns ratio, winding resistance, and oil dielectric breakdown (BDV > 50 kV) test certificates.
