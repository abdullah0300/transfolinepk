When Does a Burnt Transformer Need Rewinding?

Transformers in Karachi's SITE, Korangi, and Landhi industrial areas operate under harsh conditions — high ambient summer heat, coastal humidity, and sudden K-Electric voltage switching surges. When an electrical fault occurs, rewinding is necessary under these 3 specific scenarios:

  1. Inter-Turn Short Circuits: Overheating or voltage spikes melt the paper insulation between adjacent copper turns, causing coil burnout.
  2. Phase-to-Earth Winding Puncture: Electrical flashover between primary coils and the grounded steel core laminations.
  3. Severe Neutral Displacement Damage: Neutral coil overheating resulting from unmonitored phase load imbalance in 24/7 factory shifts.

Step-by-Step Transformer Rewinding Process

Professional transformer rewinding requires precision engineering across 6 distinct stages:

  1. Untanking & Core Inspection: The transformer top cover is unbolted, and the core-coil assembly is lifted out of the oil tank for detailed damage assessment.
  2. Stripping Burnt Coils: Burnt copper windings are carefully removed, and core lamination stacks are inspected for hotspot melting or rust.
  3. Precision Coil Winding: New primary (11 KV) and secondary (415V) coils are wound on automated winding machines using paper-insulated rectangular copper strip wire.
  4. Inter-Layer Insulation Placement: High-dielectric pressboard and Nomex paper strips are placed between turns and layers to guarantee impulse voltage strength.
  5. Assembly & Core Clamping: Freshly wound HT and LT coils are assembled onto core limbs and clamped securely to prevent acoustic vibration.
  6. Vacuum Drying & Tanking: The complete core-coil assembly is baked in a vacuum drying oven to eliminate all moisture before tanking and filling with high-BDV insulating oil.

Insulation Classes & Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI)

In Karachi's humid coastal climate, standard air-dried varnish fails prematurely. TransfoLine uses **Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI)** where assembled coils are submerged in high-grade insulating varnish under vacuum and high pressure. This forces varnish deep into every microscopic air gap, sealing out humidity and preventing moisture absorption. Learn more in our coastal oil dehydration guide.

Mandatory Post-Rewinding Routine Tests

A rewound transformer must undergo the exact same routine tests as a brand-new factory unit before dispatch to Karachi:

  • Turns Ratio Test: Verifies exact voltage ratio across all tap changer steps (+5% to -5%).
  • Winding Resistance Test: Confirms balanced resistance across all 3 phases (R, Y, B).
  • Insulation Resistance (Megger) Test: Insulation resistance between HT-to-Earth and HT-to-LT must exceed 1,000 MΩ.
  • Dielectric Breakdown Voltage (BDV): Tank insulating oil must test above 50 kV.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does transformer coil rewinding take for a Karachi factory?

Complete transformer coil rewinding and vacuum drying takes between 3 and 7 business days depending on KVA rating (100 KVA to 2000 KVA). TransfoLine provides emergency pick-up and fast-track overhaul services.

Should a burnt transformer be rewound with copper or aluminium coils?

TransfoLine exclusively recommends 99.99% pure electrolytic copper wire for rewinding Karachi industrial transformers. Copper offers superior thermal conductivity and higher resistance to short-circuit stress under 24/7 operating loads.

What tests are conducted after transformer rewinding?

Every rewound transformer undergoes Megger insulation resistance testing, turns ratio testing, winding resistance measurement, oil breakdown voltage (BDV) testing, and full load heat run testing prior to dispatch.
