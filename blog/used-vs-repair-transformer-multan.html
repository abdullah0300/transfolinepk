What Makes a Used Transformer 'Certified' at TransfoLine?

TransfoLine enforces a 6-step certification protocol for all refurbished units:

  1. Core & Winding Inspection: Complete untanking and verification of 99.99% pure electrolytic copper coils.
  2. Vapor Phase Dry-Out: High-vacuum thermal baking to remove moisture from core insulation.
  3. Gasket & Seal Replacement: Installation of new synthetic Viton oil seals and high-dielectric bushings.
  4. Fresh Oil Vacuum Processing: Filled under vacuum with fresh mineral oil (BDV > 60 kV).
  5. Pressure Leak Test: Nitrogen pressure testing to guarantee 100% leak-free operation.
  6. Routine Electrical Testing: Megger insulation resistance, turns ratio, and short-circuit loss testing. Read our Multan price & spec guide.

Burnt Copper Scrap Trade-In Credit Valuation

To reduce capital costs for Multan factory owners, TransfoLine offers an immediate scrap buyback credit:

  • Scrap Copper Valuation: The heavy copper winding weight inside your damaged unit is evaluated at live metal market rates.
  • Net Single-Invoice Billing: The scrap credit is deducted directly from the cost of your certified used replacement transformer.
  • Single Motorway Trip: Our delivery truck drops off your replacement unit and loads the old burnt unit in one trip.

When Is Workshop Coil Rewinding the Better Choice?

Workshop coil rewinding remains the ideal choice if your plant has a standby transformer on site, or if the unit is a non-standard custom step-up/step-down transformer. See our transformer repair service page.
