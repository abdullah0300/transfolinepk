🧲 Induction Furnace Engineering Considerations:

Induction furnaces generate high-order harmonics (5th, 7th, 11th, 13th) and severe electro-dynamic cyclic stresses during scrap cave-ins. TransfoLine manufactures heavy-duty furnace transformers with K-13 / K-20 rating, electrostatic Faraday shields, and Oil Forced Water Forced (OFWF) heat exchangers to prevent nuisance tripping and premature core burnouts.
