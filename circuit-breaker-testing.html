  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What is Circuit Breaker Testing?

Circuit breaker testing is the thorough functional, electrical, and mechanical testing of circuit breakers to confirm that the operating mechanism, electrical trip units, auxiliary coils, and main contact surfaces will reliably interrupt extreme short-circuit fault currents within milliseconds without contact welding, flashover, or mechanical jamming.

01

Dynamic Timing & Motion Analysis

Measures Close, Open, and Open-Close-Open (OCO) cycles. Verifies that all three phase contacts open within 2ms of each other (pole discrepancy) to prevent phase asymmetry during heavy short circuits.

02

Micro-Ohm Contact Resistance (Ductor Test)

Injects 100A to 200A DC through closed main contacts to measure micro-ohm resistance. Identifies contact pitting, carbon buildup, and spring fatigue before excessive I²R heating causes thermal runaway.

02
  SCOPE OF TESTING

Diagnostic Tests Performed on Circuit Breakers

CB.01 Operating Time & Velocity Characteristics High-speed digital timing measurement of main and auxiliary contacts during Close, Open, and Trip-Free operations. Timing Analysis
CB.02 Static & Dynamic Contact Resistance (DCRM) Four-wire Kelvin micro-ohmmeter measurement across each vacuum interrupter or arc chute assembly under high DC current. Contact Resistance
CB.03 Vacuum Bottle Dielectric Integrity (Hipot) Applying high AC test potential across open vacuum interrupter contacts to verify vacuum integrity and detect internal arcing. Vacuum Bottle Hipot
CB.04 Coil Minimum Pickup & Current Profile Testing shunt trip, closing, and undervoltage release coils to verify actuation down to 70% of rated DC control voltage. Trip Coils

04   BREAKER TESTING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Technical advice on switchgear breaker maintenance.

FREQUENCY How often should industrial circuit breakers be tested?

Main incoming Air Circuit Breakers (ACB) and 11kV Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCB) should undergo annual contact resistance and timing checks, with comprehensive mechanical lubrication every 2 to 3 years.

BRANDS Which circuit breaker manufacturers do you support?

We test and commission breakers from all global manufacturers including Schneider Electric (Masterpact, EasyPact, Evolis), ABB (SACE, VD4), Siemens (SION, 3WL/3VA), Mitsubishi, and Hyundai.
