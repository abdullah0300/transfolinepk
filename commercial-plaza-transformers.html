INDOOR SAFETY Why are Cast Resin Dry-Type transformers mandatory for indoor commercial basements in Pakistan?

Cast Resin transformers use solid vacuum-cast epoxy insulation instead of flammable mineral oil. They are certified Class F1 self-extinguishing, meaning they cannot catch fire or explode, making them safe for installation directly inside commercial plazas, shopping malls, and hospitals.

MAINTENANCE What maintenance is required for a Cast Resin Dry-Type transformer?

Unlike oil transformers that require periodic oil sampling, BDV dehydration, and gasket replacements, Cast Resin transformers require virtually zero liquid maintenance—only periodic visual checks and vacuuming of surface dust from cooling air channels.

HEAT RESISTANCE Can Cast Resin transformers handle high ambient summer heat in Pakistan?

Yes. TransfoLine Cast Resin transformers are engineered with high-temperature Class F (155°C) or Class H (180°C) insulation systems and cross-flow cooling fans that ensure reliable continuous operation even in 48°C ambient summer conditions.
