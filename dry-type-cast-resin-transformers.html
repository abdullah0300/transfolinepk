  Frequently Asked Questions

Dry-type transformer engineering FAQ.

Answers to common questions regarding Cast Resin CRT transformers, fire regulations, and maintenance in Pakistan.

SAFETY Why are dry-type transformers mandatory for basement substations?

Unlike oil-immersed units, cast resin dry-type transformers contain no combustible mineral oil. Under extreme thermal overload or external flame, the solid epoxy resin does not produce explosive gases, complying with municipal fire safety codes.

COOLING How does Forced Air (AF) cooling increase transformer capacity?

Cross-flow cooling fans mounted at the base of the coils force air through internal ventilation ducts, allowing the transformer to carry up to 40% additional load continuously during peak building hours without overheating.

MAINTENANCE What routine maintenance is required for cast resin units?

Maintenance is minimal: periodic visual inspections, vacuuming surface dust from cooling ducts during planned shutdowns, and verifying PT100 temperature controller calibration.

WARRANTY What warranty does TransfoLine provide on dry-type transformers?

Every TransfoLine dry-type transformer includes a comprehensive 12-month replacement warranty backed by our 24/7 engineering emergency response team.
