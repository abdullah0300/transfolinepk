  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What is Earth Ground Resistance Testing?

Earth ground testing is the diagnostic evaluation of an electrical grounding electrode or grid to measure its ohmic resistance to true earth mass. A low-resistance grounding path (≤ 1Ω for substations, ≤ 5Ω for industrial power panels) ensures that high fault currents and lightning surges safely dissipate into the ground without elevating chassis voltage to lethal levels.

01

3-Point Fall-of-Potential Testing

Using the IEEE 81 standard 62% rule, we inject a test current into the earth electrode and auxiliary current stake, measuring voltage drop with a potential probe across multiple distances to plot the true resistance plateau.

02

4-Point Wenner Soil Resistivity Survey

Four equally spaced electrodes inject current and measure voltage to determine soil resistivity (Ω·m) at varying depths, providing critical data for sizing new chemical earthing and grounding grid designs.

  METHODOLOGY

Grounding & Soil Diagnostic Methods

E.01 Individual Earth Pit Resistance (Fall-of-Potential) Testing isolated earth pits for distribution transformers, diesel generators, lightning down-conductors, and main LT panels. Earth Pit Test
E.02 Substation Grounding Grid Impedance & Continuity High-current ground grid continuity testing to verify that all switchgear columns, transformer tanks, and fence posts are bonded to the primary copper ground loop. Ground Grid Test
E.03 Soil Resistivity Profile (Wenner 4-Point) Multi-layer soil resistivity testing at spacing intervals from 1 meter to 30 meters for greenfield solar plants, grid stations, and industrial parks. Soil Resistivity
E.04 Step & Touch Potential Safety Analysis Calculating human safety voltage gradients during maximum ground fault conditions to prevent electric shock hazards inside substation yards. Touch/Step Potential

Frequently Asked Questions.

Expert guidance on grounding systems and soil testing.

CHEMICAL EARTHING What causes earth pit resistance to increase over time?

Soil moisture depletion during dry seasons, corrosion of copper-bonded rods, mechanical loosening of terminal clamps, and washing away of conductive salt/charcoal compounds all increase resistance over time.

REPORT Do you provide official certification for utility/insurance audits?

Yes. We deliver signed and stamped engineering test reports with exact resistance graphs, tester calibration certificates, and recommendations compliant with LESCO/K-Electric and insurance audit standards.
