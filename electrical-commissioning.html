  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What is Electrical Commissioning & Hot Energization?

Electrical commissioning is the comprehensive process of transitioning an electrical power system from the static (cold) construction phase to fully operational (hot) energized service. It validates that transformers, switchgears, protection schemes, and control automation work synchronously under live grid voltage and full factory production load.

01

Controlled Energization Protocol

Execution of a strict step-by-step energization sequence: isolating secondary breakers, racking in main HT breakers, closing grid incomers, and monitoring inrush current behavior with zero risk to upstream grid stability.

02

On-Load Step Ramping & Diagnostics

Progressive load application (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) while continuously conducting live infrared thermal scans, logging power factor, checking voltage regulation, and verifying balanced 3-phase currents.

02
  PROCESS WORKFLOW

The 4-Stage Energization & Commissioning Framework

STAGE 1 Pre-Energization Readiness & Permitting Reviewing cold test records, closing all Category A punch items, applying lockout/tagout (LOTO), and obtaining formal Permit-to-Work (PTW) and DISCO sanction. LOTO & Permits
STAGE 2 Initial Live Voltage Application (No-Load) Closing main incoming breaker, verifying transformer humming and magnetizing inrush, checking secondary busbar phase-to-phase and phase-to-neutral voltages. No-Load Energization
STAGE 3 24-Hour Soak Test & Phase Rotation Check 24-hour continuous no-load voltage soak, confirming clockwise phase sequence (RYB) at all distribution boards, and testing ATS synchronization. 24-Hr Soak Test
STAGE 4 Full Load Commissioning & Handover Sequential closing of feeder breakers, on-load FLIR thermal scanning of all bolted terminals, APFC capacitor bank tuning, and signing the Handover Certificate. Full-Load Handover

Frequently Asked Questions.

Expert guidance on plant energization and handover.

SAFETY What safety protocols are observed during energization?

We maintain strict NFPA 70E arc flash safety standards: personal protective equipment (PPE Level 4 arc suits for HT switching), exclusion safety zones, non-contact high-voltage detectors, and synchronized LOTO procedures.

POST-COMMISSIONING Do you provide post-commissioning warranty support?

Yes. Every facility commissioned by TransfoLine includes a standard post-energization thermal scan after 30 days and 24/7 technical hotline support.
