01
  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What is Generator Load Bank Testing?

Generator load bank testing is the rigorous process of applying calibrated incremental electrical loads (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, and 110% overload) to prime and standby generators. It evaluates engine combustion efficiency, alternator excitation, thermal stability, and cooling performance without risking power interruptions to facility production.

01

Elimination of Wet Stacking

Diesel generators operating below 30% load accumulate unburnt fuel in exhaust manifolds. Full load testing heats the engine to optimal combustion temperatures, burning off carbon deposits and restoring full horsepower.

02

Transient Voltage & Frequency Stability

We test sudden 100% block load application and rejection to verify that the governor and Automatic Voltage Regulator (AVR) restore stable 50Hz frequency and 400V voltage within ISO 8528 G2/G3 class limits.

02
  DIAGNOSTIC SCOPE

Key Generator & Alternator Tests We Perform

GEN.01 4-Hour Step-Load Bank Testing Step-wise loading at 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% (and 110% overload for 1 hour) while continuously monitoring engine oil pressure, coolant temperature, exhaust temps, and battery charging. Load Bank Test
GEN.02 Alternator Insulation Resistance & PI Megger testing of main stator windings, rotor field coils, and exciter stator/rotor windings with 10-minute Polarization Index (PI) calculation. Insulation Diagnostics
GEN.03 AVR Tuning & Voltage Regulation Adjustment of alternator droop, stability, and under-frequency roll-off (UFRO) to ensure steady-state voltage remains within ±0.5%. AVR Calibration
GEN.04 ATS & Synchronizing Panel Verification Testing auto-start sequence on mains failure, synchronization phase matching, reverse power relay (ANSI 32), and load sharing between multiple paralleled generators. ATS & Synchronization

Frequently Asked Questions.

Technical advice on standby generator reliability.

FREQUENCY How often should emergency generators undergo load bank testing?

Standby generators in hospitals, data centers, and manufacturing plants should undergo annual load bank testing per NFPA 110 guidelines to ensure full load capacity and prevent engine carbon fouling.

BRANDS Which generator and engine brands do you service?

We test and commission all major diesel and gas generators powered by Cummins, Perkins, Caterpillar, MTU, FG Wilson, Kohler, Volvo Penta, and alternators by Stamford, Leroy Somer, and Mecc Alte.
