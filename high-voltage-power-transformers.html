  Frequently Asked Questions

High-voltage power transformer FAQ.

Guidance on large MVA power transformers, tap changers, and 132kV grid connections.

VOLTAGE What is the advantage of an On-Load Tap Changer (OLTC)?

An OLTC adjusts transformer turns ratios automatically while energizing load, counteracting utility grid voltage drops during peak hours to ensure steady 415V power to sensitive plant machinery.

DIAGNOSTICS What testing is required before energizing a 10 MVA transformer?

Standard pre-commissioning testing includes SFRA (Sweep Frequency Response Analysis), Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA), Tan Delta (C&DF), winding resistance, and transformer turns ratio (TTR).

PROTECTION What fire protection systems are used on large MVA transformers?

TransfoLine installs automatic Nitrogen Injection Fire Protection Systems (NIFPS), deluge water spray networks, and oil soak pit drainage systems to prevent catastrophic substation fires.

WARRANTY What warranty is included on TransfoLine power transformers?

All new and remanufactured power transformers include a comprehensive 12-month operational warranty with guaranteed 24/7 on-site emergency field support.
