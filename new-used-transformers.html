04   Answers

Questions about buying transformers.

Common questions about transformer sales. Call us for anything else.

CAPACITY What capacity range of transformers do you supply?

We supply new and certified-used distribution and power transformers from 25 kVA to 10 MVA (10,000 kVA) in both oil-immersed (ONAN/ONAF) and cast resin dry-type configurations, fully compliant with WAPDA P-10 and DISCO standards.

TESTING Are your used transformers tested and certified?

Yes. Every used transformer goes through our rigorous 15-point diagnostic testing procedure (insulation resistance, turns ratio, oil BDV ≥ 50kV, and winding resistance) and comes backed by a comprehensive warranty at 25–35% below new equipment prices.

BUYBACK Do you purchase old or decommissioned transformers?

Yes. We purchase used, redundant, or faulty transformers of all sizes (25 kVA to 10 MVA) and brands at competitive wholesale market rates, with on-site inspection and immediate payment.

DISCOUNTS What discounts do you offer on bulk transformer orders?

Because you purchase directly from TransfoLine with zero middlemen or distributor markups, we provide tiered volume discounts for housing societies, commercial developers, and industrial electrification projects.
