📉 Power Factor Formula & Penalty Surcharge Mechanism:

Under NEPRA regulations, when an industrial consumer's power factor drops below 0.90, DISCOs apply a penalty surcharge of up to 1.5% to 2% of the total monthly bill for every 0.01 drop. Installing a custom automatic PFI panel typically amortizes 100% of its cost in 3 to 5 months.
