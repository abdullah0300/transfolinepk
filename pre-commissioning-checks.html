  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What are Electrical Pre-Commissioning Checks?

Pre-commissioning electrical checks are the systematic physical, mechanical, and de-energized electrical inspections performed on switchboards, transformers, motor control centers, and cabling before applying live grid voltage. These cold checks eliminate wiring misconnections, loose busbar bolts, incorrect CT ratios, and phase reversals that could cause instant short-circuit explosions upon energization.

01

Calibrated Bolt Torque Verification

Using digital torque wrenches, every busbar splice, transformer terminal lug, and breaker connection is torqued to specified Newton-meter (N·m) values and marked with tamper-evident torque paint.

02

Point-to-Point Wiring Ring-Out

Every control wire, trip circuit, interlock link, and CT/PT secondary cable is physically checked against the approved 'As-Built' schematic drawings to guarantee zero miswiring errors.

02
  INSPECTION SCOPE

Standard Pre-Commissioning Inspection Checklist

PRE.01 Mechanical & Visual Inspection Checking physical anchoring, panel alignment, IP ingress door seals, silica gel breather condition, and removal of all foreign tools/debris from switchgear enclosures. Visual Inspection
PRE.02 Phase Identification & Sequence Verification Red-Yellow-Blue (RYB) color identification, neutral/earth segregation check, and verification of consistent clockwise phase rotation across all incoming and outgoing feeders. Phase Sequence
PRE.03 CT / PT Secondary Circuit Checks Checking Current Transformer (CT) polarity, burden, ratio verification, and ensuring single-point grounding of all CT secondary star points. CT / PT Checks
PRE.04 Mechanical Interlock & Racking Checks Testing breaker test/service racking mechanisms, door safety interlocks, and Castell mechanical key interlocking between mains and bus couplers. Interlock Check

04   PRE-COMMISSIONING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Expert guidance on cold electrical inspection.

DELIVERABLES What documentation is provided after pre-commissioning?

You receive a formal signed Pre-Commissioning Certificate, complete NETA ATS punch list, torque verification log sheets with torque marks, and red-line markup drawings ready for energization authority submission.

PUNCH LIST What happens if defects are discovered during cold checks?

Defects are cataloged in a prioritized Punch List (Category A: Safety critical, Category B: Operational non-critical). Our team rectifies loose connections, re-labels ferrules, and coordinates immediate resolution before greenlighting energization.
