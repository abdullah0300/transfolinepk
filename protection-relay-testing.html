  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What is Protection Relay Testing?

Protection relay testing is the precision testing of electrical protective relays by injecting simulated fault currents and system voltages to confirm that the relay correctly detects electrical faults (overcurrent, short-circuit, earth fault, reverse power, or differential unbalance) and signals the circuit breaker to trip within exact millisecond tolerances.

01

Secondary Injection Testing

Using 3-phase OMICRON CMC test sets, we inject calibrated currents and phase-shifted voltages directly into relay inputs to verify pickup thresholds, dropout ratios, reset times, and inverse definite minimum time (IDMT) operating curves.

02

Scheme & Trip Circuit Validation

We test the end-to-end trip scheme—from relay output contact actuation, through auxiliary tripping relays (Master Trip 86), up to the circuit breaker trip coil to ensure 100% reliable physical breaker opening.

  PROTECTION FUNCTIONS

ANSI Protection Functions We Test & Calibrate

ANSI 50/51 Instantaneous & Time-Overcurrent Protection Verification of phase overcurrent pickup and IDMT curves (IEC Normal Inverse, Very Inverse, Extremely Inverse) to guarantee proper discrimination. Overcurrent (50/51)
ANSI 50N/51N Earth Fault & Sensitive Earth Fault (SEF) Testing ground fault detection thresholds and directional earth fault (67N) sensing for solidly grounded and resistance-grounded systems. Earth Fault (51N)
ANSI 87T/87G Transformer & Generator Differential Protection Restrained percentage differential testing, harmonic restraint (2nd & 5th harmonic blocking for inrush/overexcitation), and high-set instantaneous trip verification. Differential (87T)
ANSI 27/59 Under-Voltage & Over-Voltage Protection Voltage magnitude thresholds, drop-off timing, and neutral voltage displacement (59N) checks to protect motors and transformers from grid fluctuations. Voltage (27/59)
ANSI 32/40 Reverse Power & Loss of Field Protection Critical testing for captive generator synchronization panels to prevent generator motoring and loss of excitation damage. Generator Protection

04   RELAY TESTING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Expert answers regarding relay coordination and injection testing.

FREQUENCY How often should protection relays be tested?

Numerical relays should undergo secondary injection testing every 2 to 3 years. Electromechanical and static relays should be calibrated annually because spring tensions and internal electronic components drift over time.

COORDINATION Can TransfoLine help with relay coordination settings?

Yes. Our protection engineers perform full discrimination and relay coordination studies using ETAP/DigSILENT to calculate optimal pickup and time multiplier settings (TMS) before on-site injection testing.
