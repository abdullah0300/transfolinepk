  Frequently Asked Questions

Solar substation engineering FAQ.

Answers to critical questions regarding solar inverter transformers, net-metering, and grid protection in Pakistan.

HARMONICS How do solar inverter harmonics affect transformers?

High-frequency pulse-width modulation (PWM) switching produces harmonic currents (especially 5th, 7th, 11th, and 13th orders) that increase core eddy losses and coil hot spots. TransfoLine uses K-factor reinforced conductors and electrostatic copper shields to eliminate harmonic overheating.

NET-METERING What are the DISCO requirements for 11kV industrial solar net-metering?

DISCOs require a dedicated 11kV VCB switchgear with calibrated PT/CT metering units, bi-directional 4-quadrant green meters, zero-export controllers (if unapproved for export), and anti-islanding protection relays (IEEE 1547 compliant).

PAYBACK What is the payback period for an industrial solar substation in Pakistan?

At current Pakistani industrial grid tariffs (PKR 42–55 per kWh), a 1 MW solar plant equipped with a dedicated TransfoLine step-up transformer achieves full capital payback within 2.8 to 3.2 years.

WARRANTY What warranty does TransfoLine provide on solar transformers?

Every TransfoLine solar inverter-duty transformer is backed by a 12-month complete replacement warranty and 24/7 on-site emergency engineering field support.
