  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What is electrical testing and commissioning?

Electrical testing and commissioning is the systematic engineering process of verifying, inspecting, and testing every component of an electrical installation—including transformers, switchgears, protection relays, cables, and grounding networks—to ensure full compliance with international standards (IEC 60076, IEEE, NETA) and safe energization without downtime.

Our 16 Testing & Commissioning Capabilities

Explore our specialized diagnostic and commissioning divisions designed for industrial, utility, and commercial applications.

SVC-T01 · PRIMARY

Transformer Testing

Insulation resistance, turns ratio (TTR), SFRA, winding resistance, oil dielectric strength, power factor & loss measurements.

 SVC-T02 · PRIORITY

Insulation Resistance Testing

Megger testing for transformers, motors, generators, cables, and switchgears to detect insulation breakdown.

 SVC-T03 · HIGH TENSION

High Voltage (HV) Testing

AC/DC Hipot, VLF withstand testing, partial discharge (PD) detection, and lightning impulse withstand verification.

 SVC-T04 · PROTECTION

Protection Relay Testing

OMICRON CMC 256 & Megger secondary injection testing for numerical, static, and electromechanical relays.

 SVC-T05 · SWITCHGEAR

Circuit Breaker Testing

Timing tests, contact resistance, primary & secondary injection for LV, MV, and HV vacuum and SF6 breakers.

 SVC-T06 · INFRARED

Thermal Imaging Inspection

FLIR & Testo infrared thermography to detect abnormal hotspots on busbars, panels, transformers, and joints.

 SVC-T07 · POWER QUALITY

Power Quality Analysis

Harmonics analysis, voltage sag/swell monitoring, power factor evaluation, and load profiling with Fluke analyzers.

 SVC-T08 · GENERATION

Generator & Load Bank Testing

Full load bank testing, excitation check, voltage regulation, vibration analysis, and insulation diagnostics.

 SVC-T09 · CABLES

Underground Cable Testing

VLF Hipot, TDR fault locating, Tan Delta, partial discharge, sheath integrity, and cable route tracing.

 SVC-T10 · DRIVES

Motor & Load Testing

Insulation resistance, winding resistance, torque measurement, full-load evaluation, and vibration analysis.

 SVC-T11 · GROUNDING

Earth Ground Resistance Testing

Fall-of-potential testing, clamp ground measurement, soil resistivity surveys, and lightning protection verification.

 SVC-T12 · AUTOMATION

Control System Testing

PLC/SCADA functional verification, I/O loop checks, sequence logic validation, and safety interlock tests.

 SVC-T13 · PRE-COMMISSIONING

Pre-Commissioning Checks

Mechanical inspection, torque verification, phase rotation, continuity, and interlock checks before energization.

 SVC-T14 · COMMISSIONING

Electrical Commissioning

System energization, synchronized load testing, relay coordination validation, and final handover reporting.

 SVC-T15 · INTEGRITY

Continuity Testing

Busbar continuity, control panel loop checks, contact resistance, and conductor integrity verification.

05   ENGINEERING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

Key questions answered by our testing and commissioning engineers.

GENERAL What is electrical testing and commissioning?

It is the formal technical verification of electrical systems to ensure that all equipment operates safely, meets manufacturer specifications, and complies with IEC/IEEE standards before and during service.

EQUIPMENT What instruments does TransfoLine use?

We utilize calibrated precision instruments from Megger (insulation & relay test sets), OMICRON (secondary injection), FLIR (thermal imaging), and Fluke (power quality analyzers and multi-meters).

LOCATION Do you provide testing across all Pakistani cities?

Yes. We mobilize testing teams across Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, and Gilgit-Baltistan with complete mobile testing units.

REPORTING How soon do we get the test certificate and report?

Preliminary on-site findings are provided immediately upon test completion. Full signed and stamped engineering reports with graphical data and recommendations are delivered within 24 to 48 hours.
