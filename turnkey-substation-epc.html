  Engineering Capabilities

Comprehensive technical execution scope.

01

Load Audit & SLD Engineering

Short-circuit calculations, busbar sizing, single-line diagrams (SLD), and protection relay coordination curves prepared per IEEE 141 and IEC 60909.

02

Transformer & Switchgear Supply

Manufacturing and delivery of 25 kVA to 10 MVA oil-immersed or cast resin dry-type transformers, 11kV HT Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) panels, and LT switchboards.

03

Civil Pad & Chemical Earth Grid

Reinforced transformer civil foundation pad, oil containment soak pit, and deep-bored chemical copper earth pit mesh achieving ground resistance ≤ 1Ω per IEEE 80.

04

Testing, Commissioning & Grid Link

Complete NETA pre-commissioning testing (Megger, Hipot, TTR, OMICRON relay injection, BDV), DISCO inspection liaison, meter sealing, and live grid energization.

03
  Execution Procedure

From concept to energized power.

A systematic 4-step engineering roadmap delivering transparent timelines and guaranteed performance.

  1. 01 Day 1–2

    Site Survey & Load Audit

    Our senior electrical engineers inspect your industrial site, analyze connected load, and design the optimal substation SLD.

  2. 02 Week 1–2

    Civil Works & Equipment Build

    While transformer and HT/LT panels are fabricated and tested in our factory, on-site civil pad and earth pit boring are completed.

  3. 03 Week 3

    Installation & Busbar Integration

    Equipment is mounted, 11kV HT cables are terminated, and copper busbars are coupled with precision torque verification.

  4. 04 Week 4

    Testing, Inspection & Energization

    Comprehensive pre-commissioning diagnostic tests are executed, utility inspection is completed, and power is switched on safely.

04   Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Common questions about this service. Call our engineering team for anything else.

EPC SCOPE What is included in a Turnkey 11kV Substation EPC contract with TransfoLine?

Our turnkey scope includes complete electrical engineering design, transformer manufacturing/supply (25 kVA–10 MVA), HT Vacuum Circuit Breaker (VCB) panels, LT distribution switchgear, PFI plants, deep-bored earthing grid (≤ 1Ω), 11kV cabling, civil pad works, pre-commissioning testing, and WAPDA/DISCO grid energization under a single warranty.

TIMELINE How long does it take to design, install, and energize an industrial substation?

Standard distribution substations (200 kVA to 1,000 kVA) are completed and energized within 14 to 21 working days. Large industrial substations (1.5 MVA to 5 MVA) require 25 to 35 working days depending on utility approval lead times.

APPROVALS Does TransfoLine handle DISCO (LESCO, K-Electric, FESCO, MEPCO) utility approvals?

Yes. Our engineering team prepares complete Single Line Diagrams (SLD), protection relay coordination curves, transformer type-test certificates, and earthing reports required for rapid utility inspection and meter sealing.
