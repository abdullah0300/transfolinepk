  EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What is Underground Cable Fault Location & VLF Testing?

Underground cable testing is the specialized detection of short-circuit, open-circuit, earth fault, or high-resistance insulation breakdowns in buried medium and low-voltage power cables. By combining Time Domain Reflectometry (TDR) with electromagnetic/acoustic ground tracing and 0.1Hz Very Low Frequency (VLF) Hipot testing, we confirm cable health and pinpoint exact excavation spots.

01

TDR Radar Pre-Location

Transmits low-voltage pulses down the cable and captures impedance reflection signatures. Calculates the exact distance to the fault location in meters within seconds.

02

Acoustic Thumper Pinpointing

Discharges synchronized capacitive high-voltage surges into the fault point, generating an underground acoustic discharge detected on the ground surface with high-gain seismic microphones.

02
  SCOPE OF TESTING

Diagnostic Tests for Underground Cable Networks

CAB.01 0.1Hz VLF AC Withstand (Hipot) Testing Applying sinusoidal 0.1Hz AC test voltages up to 3x nominal phase voltage for 15 to 60 minutes per IEEE 400.2 to prove cable insulation integrity without damage. VLF Hipot
CAB.02 TDR & Arc Reflection Fault Pinpointing Locating high-resistance flashover faults in underground 11kV cables using Arc Reflection Method (ARM) and high-energy acoustic surface sensors. Fault Location
CAB.03 Outer Sheath Integrity Testing (DC Step Voltage) Applying DC potential to the metallic screen to detect water penetration pinholes in the outer PVC/PE sheath using ground step voltage probes. Sheath Testing
CAB.04 Insulation Resistance & Polarization Index 5kV DC Megger testing between core-to-core and core-to-earth shields to establish baseline dielectric absorption values before energization. Megger Test

Frequently Asked Questions.

Expert guidance on underground power cable testing.

REPAIR Can TransfoLine repair the cable after pinpointing the fault?

Yes. In addition to fault location, our high-voltage cable jointers execute 11kV/33kV heat-shrinkable or cold-shrinkable straight-through joints and terminations, followed by a post-repair VLF proof test.

ACCURACY What is the typical accuracy of your acoustic fault pinpointing?

Our digital acoustic ground receivers pinpoint the fault to within ±10 centimeters directly above the buried cable, requiring an excavation trench of less than 1 square meter.
