Frequently asked questions.

Common questions about this service. Call our engineering team for anything else.

COMPLIANCE What is the difference between a WAPDA approved transformer and a private industrial transformer?

A WAPDA-approved transformer strictly meets WAPDA Specification P-10 / DDS-84 loss thresholds, BIL levels (75kV), and specific core/coil designs required for grid interconnection. Non-compliant units are rejected during DISCO inspection.

LIAISON Can TransfoLine help get my transformer approved by LESCO or K-Electric?

Yes. Every transformer supplied by TransfoLine comes with complete certified type-test dossiers, loss calculation sheets, and single line diagrams (SLD) guaranteed to meet utility approval guidelines.

RATINGS What are the standard kVA ratings under WAPDA Specification P-10?

Standard 3-phase ratings under WAPDA P-10 include 25 kVA, 50 kVA, 100 kVA, 200 kVA, 400 kVA, and 630 kVA. Higher capacities (1,000 kVA to 10 MVA) are built to custom DISCO industrial sub-station specifications.
